Web Developer Workflow

Follow these steps from learning development to delivering your first client website.

1
💻

Learn Web Development

Start with HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Learn responsive design, Git and basic web development fundamentals.

2
🛠️

Build Real Projects

Create business websites, portfolios, landing pages, e-commerce demos and useful web applications.

3
🌐

Create Your Portfolio

Build a professional portfolio showing your projects, skills, services and contact information.

4
☁️

Deploy Your Projects

Put your websites online so potential clients can view working demos instead of only screenshots.

5
🔍

Find Businesses

Look for businesses that have no website or an outdated website and could benefit from a stronger online presence.

6
🎨

Create a Demo

Create a small demo showing what the business website could look like. Keep demo content clearly labelled.

7
📩

Contact the Business

Introduce yourself professionally and share the live demo so the owner can experience the website.

8
🤝

Agree Scope & Price

Clearly define pages, features, revisions, domain, hosting, maintenance, timeline and total project price.

9
💳

Take an Advance

Once the project is agreed, take an advance before beginning the full client-specific customisation.

10
⚙️

Build & Customise

Add the client's actual branding, content, images, contact information and agreed functionality.

11
🧪

Test Everything

Test mobile responsiveness, links, forms, WhatsApp, calls, performance, SEO and browser compatibility.

12
🚀

Deploy & Deliver

Get final approval, collect the remaining payment and deploy the production website with the agreed domain and hosting.

13
⭐

Get a Testimonial

After successful delivery, ask the client for a genuine testimonial and permission to showcase the project.

14
🔄

Repeat & Grow

Add the project to your portfolio, find your next client and gradually increase your pricing and services.

Important Rules

Protect your time and keep client work professional.

👀

Demo ≠ Delivery

A free demo is for showing your concept. The complete customised website is the paid product.

💰

Agree Before Building

Define the price, scope, revisions and payment terms before doing substantial client-specific work.

📦

Professional Handover

After payment and approval, deliver the agreed website, accounts, documentation and access.
